## 🧠 About Me

- 💻 I build **modern, accessible interfaces** with careful attention to motion design and micro-interactions
- 🤖 I work with **AI solutions & integration** — connecting LLMs, automation and real products
- 📊 My background in **Business & Administration** helps me bridge the gap between strategy and technical execution
- 🚀 Currently building a **marketplace product** end-to-end with a small team — from design system to deploy
- 🎯 My goal: ship products that create **real impact**, combining technology, design and business value
